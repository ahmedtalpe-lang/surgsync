NCEPOD Classification Key

NCEPOD 1
Immediate (Minutes)
Life, limb, or organ-saving intervention. Simultaneous resuscitation and surgery (e.g., Trauma laparotomy, ruptured AAA).
NCEPOD 2
Urgent (Hours)
Acute onset or clinical deterioration (e.g., Acute appendicitis, strangulated hernia, perforated viscus).
NCEPOD 3
Expedited (Days)
Condition requiring early treatment where not an immediate threat to life or organ survival.

  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
